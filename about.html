About EventFest

EventFest is our college's flagship annual celebration, bringing together students from every branch and year for three days of technical competitions, cultural performances, and community building.

Since its founding, the event has grown into one of the most anticipated gatherings on campus, hosted entirely by student volunteers and supported by the college administration. Whether you're here to compete, perform, or simply celebrate campus life, there's something for everyone.
